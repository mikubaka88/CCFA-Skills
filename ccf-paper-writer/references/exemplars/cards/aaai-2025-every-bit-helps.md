## Story Pattern

**Source observation:** The abstract defines matching with ordinal information and distortion, recalls the known two-query result, and introduces bounds for a general constant query budget. A previously known lower bound establishes the sense in which the new guarantee is optimal; the abstract then states the extension and open questions resolved.

## Abstract Moves

**Writing observation in the inspected passage:** The result is explained through the parameters and earlier bounds needed to understand its significance. Its support is theoretical, not an implied experimental comparison.

## Reusable Techniques

**Adaptation advice for matching work:**

- Define the model and quantity that make a bound interpretable.
- Explain exactly which earlier result or lower bound makes a theorem consequential.
- Retain several connected theoretical results when needed; empirical evaluation is not a required substitute for proof.

## Do-Not-Copy Boundary

Use the target study's own definitions, mechanisms, conditions, results, and claims. Common academic phrasing and established terms may be reused when they fit; distinctive source sentences and scientific conclusions may not be transplanted.
