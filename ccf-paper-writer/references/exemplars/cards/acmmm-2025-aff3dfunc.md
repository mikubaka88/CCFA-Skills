## Story Pattern

**Source observation:** The introduction defines affordance through possible interactions and then narrows the task to grounding textual descriptions in 3D point-cloud regions. The abstract describes functional text enhancement followed by semantic–geometric alignment and reports evaluations involving text-query variation and robots.

## Abstract Moves

**Writing observation in the inspected passage:** The useful writing move is the connection from an action-related concept to the region predicted by the model. Broad promotional phrases elsewhere in the abstract are not a vocabulary model.

## Reusable Techniques

**Adaptation advice for matching work:**

- Define a task through what its output means for the scientific question.
- Explain the information supplied by text and geometry before describing their alignment.
- Use downstream demonstrations when the current claims require them; robot evaluation is not a universal requirement.

## Do-Not-Copy Boundary

Use the target study's own definitions, mechanisms, conditions, results, and claims. Common academic phrasing and established terms may be reused when they fit; distinctive source sentences and scientific conclusions may not be transplanted.
