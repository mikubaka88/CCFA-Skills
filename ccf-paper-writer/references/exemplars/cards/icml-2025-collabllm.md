Venue/year: ICML 2025.
Source: PMLR `https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/wu25i.html`.
Use when: writing LLM, agent, human-AI collaboration, multiturn interaction, reward design, or user-study papers.

Inspected writing: Stored original text `references/exemplars/papers/icml-2025-collabllm.md`, Abstract and opening Introduction.

This card navigates selected original passages. The source observations below are distinct from the adaptation advice; read the relevant original when using it.

## Story Pattern

**Source observation:** The abstract connects next-turn rewards to long-term interaction, introduces simulated continuations for estimating a response's contribution, and distinguishes benchmark results from a user study. The introduction grounds the problem in users refining initially incomplete requests.

## Abstract Moves

**Writing observation in the inspected passage:** The response objective, interaction behavior, and user outcome occupy different explanatory roles. Their relationship supplies the argument; their metrics are not interchangeable.

## Reusable Techniques

**Adaptation advice for matching work:**

- Explain the time horizon of the objective and the behavior it is intended to encourage.
- Separate model-judged benchmark findings from human-study findings.
- Keep multiple related contributions visible while preserving the actual source of each result.

## Do-Not-Copy Boundary

Use the target study's own definitions, mechanisms, conditions, results, and claims. Common academic phrasing and established terms may be reused when they fit; distinctive source sentences and scientific conclusions may not be transplanted.
