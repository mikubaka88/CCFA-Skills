# Programming languages reference papers

## Quotient Polymorphism

POPL 2026 · Distinguished Paper and 25-minute research talk

Brandon Hewer, Graham Hutton

Start with the introductory motivating example. Follow the transition from the standard abstraction to quotient types and the proof obligations that accompany the generalization.

[Program or award](https://popl26.sigplan.org/track/POPL-2026-popl-research-papers) · [Proceedings](https://popl26.sigplan.org/details/POPL-2026-popl-research-papers/25/Quotient-Polymorphism) · [Paper](https://nottingham-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/56966982) · [Repository PDF](popl-2026-quotient-polymorphism.pdf) · [PDF source](https://people.cs.nott.ac.uk/pszgmh/quotient-poly.pdf) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Practical Type Inference with Levels

PLDI 2025 · Distinguished Paper and 20-minute research talk

Andong Fan, Han Xu, Ningning Xie

Read the abstract, motivating type examples and algorithm discussion in sequence. Locate the invariant that makes a practical implementation strategy suitable for the formal argument.

[Program or award](https://pldi25.sigplan.org/details/pldi-2025-papers/89/Practical-Type-Inference-with-Levels) · [Paper](https://sec-r.github.io/papers/pldi25level.pdf) · [Repository PDF](pldi-2025-practical-type-inference-levels.pdf) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Profiling Programming Language Learning

OOPSLA 2024 · Distinguished Paper and 20-minute research talk

Will Crichton, Shriram Krishnamurthi

Start with the framing and study sections. Examine why the authors formulate learning difficulty as something to profile and how they connect observations to the specific language being examined.
