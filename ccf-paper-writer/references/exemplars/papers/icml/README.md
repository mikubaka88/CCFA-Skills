# Machine learning reference papers

## A Random Matrix Theory Perspective on the Consistency of Diffusion Models

ICML 2026 · Oral and Outstanding Paper Honorable Mention

Binxu Wang, Jacob A. Zavatone-Veth, Cengiz Pehlevan

Use the proceedings paper to inspect how a tractable random-matrix model is connected to the consistency question and which predictions are tested in more realistic settings. The awards list uses a shorter title.

[Program or award](https://blog.icml.cc/2026/07/05/announcing-the-icml-2026-awards/) · [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02908) · [Repository PDF](icml-2026-random-matrix-diffusion-arxiv-v2.pdf) · [PDF source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02908v2) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Transformers are Inherently Succinct

ICLR 2026 · Outstanding Paper

Pascal Bergsträßer, Ryan Cotterell, Anthony Widjaja Lin

Start with the abstract and introductory definitions. Follow the distinction between expressiveness and succinctness, then identify the size measure and model assumptions that make the comparison meaningful.

[Program or award](https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/04/23/announcing-the-iclr-2026-outstanding-papers/) · [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/5f7804e8855efe5554025217abc49315-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19315) · [Repository PDF](../iclr-2026-transformers-succinct.pdf)

## Superposition Yields Robust Neural Scaling

NeurIPS 2025 · Best Paper Runner-Up, presented in an oral session

Yizhou Liu, Ziming Liu, Jeff Gore

Read the abstract and toy-model setup to see how the paper turns a scaling-law observation into a candidate mechanism, then uses a controlled change in superposition to distinguish regimes.

[Program or award](https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/11/26/announcing-the-neurips-2025-best-paper-awards/) · [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/e97ac22927560eb2de6b658498cbc575-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10465) · [Repository PDF](neurips-2025-superposition-neural-scaling-arxiv-v4.pdf) · [PDF source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10465v4) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
